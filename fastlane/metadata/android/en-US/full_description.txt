Every Android launcher hands you a grid of apps and asks you to choose again, every time you press Home. Gretel asks once.

Gretel is an anti-launcher. Pick the app you actually want to use, make Gretel your home app, and that app becomes the place your device always returns to. No grid, no feed, nothing else competing for your attention.

MADE FOR ONE-PURPOSE DEVICES
Gretel was built for e-readers, where a reading app such as KOReader should simply be the device. It works just as well on a spare phone or tablet you want to turn into a distraction-free reading, writing, reference, or dashboard tool.

BUILT FOR E-INK
High-contrast black and white, large touch targets, no ripples, and no animation. Every screen is paged, never scrolled, and e-reader page buttons turn the pages. Choose an automatic, white, or true black theme.

NEVER STUCK
Press Home once to return to your chosen app. Press Home twice quickly to open Gretel's settings. The double-Home timing can be set to quick, normal, or relaxed. You can change your app, pick another home screen in Android settings, or uninstall Gretel at any time.

SWITCHES YOU CONTROL
Gretel can reopen your app after a restart, and bring it back when the app exits. Both behaviors are settings you can turn off. If the app keeps crashing, Gretel stops reopening it and shows its settings instead.

NOT A KIOSK
Gretel does not use Device Admin, Accessibility services, screen overlays, or lock task mode. It is a home app, not a cage. If you need to stop someone from leaving an app, Gretel is the wrong tool.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION
No accounts, analytics, ads, crash reporters, or network permission. Gretel does not request access to your full app list. Your chosen app and settings never leave the device.

OPEN SOURCE
Apache License 2.0. Source, issues, and signed builds are at https://github.com/abeant/gretel

Works on Android 8.0 and newer, on any device where Android lets you choose the home app. English and Spanish.
